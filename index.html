Stella Shop Scheduler: Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

Stella Shop Scheduler is a private tool. Stella Harvey uses it to publish posts to her own Facebook Page and to read that Page's statistics. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it uses

With Stella's permission, it uses Meta's Pages API to publish videos and posts to her Page and to read the Page's totals for views, reach and engagement.

What it does not do

It does not collect, store, sell or share personal information about people who visit or follow the Page. It does not read private messages.

What is kept

The access key for the Page stays on the owner's own computer. Page statistics are kept as totals for the owner's own reports.

Removing access and deleting data

Stella can remove the tool's access at any time in her Facebook settings, under Business integrations. To ask a question or to request deletion of any data, message Stella Harvey through her Facebook Page.
